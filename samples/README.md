# Samples

The examples are intentionally localized.

## Spanish

Spanish examples are Mexico-first.

They may include context such as:

- RFC
- IVA
- CDMX
- S.A. de C.V.
- Mexican business and legal wording

`S.A. de C.V.` is intentional and should not be treated as a typo. It is common
Mexican company wording.

## English, Hindi, and Punjabi

The built-in examples in `app.js` are India-first.

They may include context such as:

- WhatsApp, Excel, and Gmail workflows
- Gurugram and Mohali
- INR / Rs
- GST
- GSTIN
- CIN
- Private Limited companies

The next regional example packs may target USA, UK, Australia, China, French,
German, Japanese, and Portuguese-speaking audiences.
